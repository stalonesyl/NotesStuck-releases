# Notes Stuck

**Sticky notes for your Windows desktop that don't get lost.**

Notes Stuck sits in your system tray and stays out of the way until you need it. Press a shortcut, type, and you're done. Your note stays stuck to your screen until you put it away, and it's still there tomorrow when you sign back in.

## What it's for

**You need to jot something down, right now.**
You're on a call and someone reads out a phone number. A thought hits you halfway through something else. Press **Ctrl+Alt+N** from any app and a note pops up under your cursor, ready to type in. No app to find, no file to name, nothing to save.

**You want something in front of you all day.**
Today's three priorities. The order number you keep needing. The Wi-Fi password for a guest. Pin it on top of your other windows and it stays where you can see it.

**You keep forgetting the thing.**
"Call the dentist at 3." "Move the laundry." "Send the invoice on Friday." Give a note a reminder (in an hour, this evening, tomorrow, or any time you type), and it pops back up with a notification when it's due. You can put a reminder on one line of a checklist too, so a single list can hold the whole day.

**You're working through a list.**
Groceries, packing, a pre-launch checklist, the steps for something you only do once a year. Turn any note into a checklist and tick things off as you go.

**You want your desk clean, without losing anything.**
**Ctrl+Alt+S** hides every note at once, for screen-sharing or just some focus, and brings them all back when you press it again. Close a note and it isn't gone, only tucked away in **All notes**. Archive the ones you're done with. Deleted notes wait in the trash in case you change your mind.

**You have a lot of notes about a lot of things.**
Type a tag like `#work`, `#gift-ideas` or `#house` anywhere in a note. Every tag gets its own color, and **All notes** can show just one tag, so the scattered bits about the same thing end up together. Search finds any note by its text.

**You changed a note and wish you hadn't.**
Every note keeps its own history. Look back at an earlier version, see what changed side by side, and restore it if you like.

## A few nice touches

- **One place for everything.** *All notes* shows every note as cards or a list, with your tags, your reminders as an agenda, archived notes and the trash.
- **Light or dark.** It follows your Windows theme, or you can choose one.
- **Starts with Windows**, if you want it to, so your notes are already up when you sign in.
- **Saves as you type.** There's no Save button because you never need one.
- **Your notes stay on your PC.** No account and no cloud.
- **Updates itself.** New versions download quietly and switch over the next time the app restarts. You never have to reinstall.

## Shortcuts worth knowing

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+Alt+N** | New note, from anywhere |
| **Ctrl+Alt+S** | Hide or show all notes |
| **Ctrl+Shift+C** | Make a checklist |
| **Ctrl+Shift+L** | Bullet list |
| **Ctrl+B / I / U** | Bold, italic, underline |
| **Ctrl+H** | Note history |

## Installing

Download **[NotesStuck-win-Setup.exe](https://github.com/stalonesyl/NotesStuck-releases/releases/latest/download/NotesStuck-win-Setup.exe)** and run it. It installs just for you, without admin rights, and opens when it's done. It runs on Windows 10 and 11.

The installer isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" screen. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
